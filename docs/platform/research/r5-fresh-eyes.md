## Strongest unanswered objection from each role

**Cost-conscious owner — what fraction of the promised archive can actually be retained inside the priced disk, and which implementation am I buying first?**

The proposal responsibly labels the 100,000-game corpus as a test envelope. It nevertheless leaves the measured saved-study size disconnected from its 30 GB total disk model. At the single measured fixture size, one placed snapshot plus its compressed analysis is 433,095 bytes: 1,000 such pairs are 0.433 GB, 10,000 are 4.331 GB and 100,000 are **43.310 GB**, before game records, indexes, WAL, checkpoints, multiple revisions, temporary save generations and backups. This is a sensitivity calculation, not an assertion that every imported game gets a snapshot or that this fixture is representative. It shows why the fraction of imported games becoming saved studies is load-bearing.

Add a compact retained-byte sensitivity and an explicit first-slice decision: local view save/restore, estimated 4–7 engineer-days, followed by review of measured user value and storage. Make clear that the six listed ranges sum to 28–54 engineer-days while still excluding public signup/recovery and polished product work. The ranges are already qualified, but a reader should not have to total them or find the caveat in another round. The $25 model is credible as a low-occupancy hypothesis; it is not yet a priced archive entitlement.

**Implementer — when may a saved view be safely edited, and what does “saved” promise on the first supported devices?**

The immutable manifest and atomic `study_head` update are good boundaries. The checkpoint example is deliberately descriptive rather than a real schema, and the browser/device/retention/export choice is still open. Those are acceptable for a research proposal, but the first handoff should name its support contract and visible behavior. A compatible view-only restore must not present an ordinary explore action that silently discards an old branch or triggers an unexplained rebuild. An acknowledged IndexedDB transaction also needs product language distinguishing local retention from hosted backup.

Before implementation, define the first browser/device matrix, the unavailable-editing/rebuild state and an export or explicit local-only retention decision. Keep editable recovery as its separately estimated slice. During implementation, replace placeholder checkpoint collection shapes with executable validation and run the existing parent-result-change fixture. This is a small missing handoff decision, not grounds to implement the entire worker platform first.

**Security/recovery operator — which independent authority proves that every acknowledged revocation survived loss of the host, and which authority stops paid tasks while that host is gone?**

The prose specifies sound outcomes: deny before acknowledgment, retain an independent journal, fail closed without coverage and run an independent reaper. It does not yet select their stores, principals, cutoff evidence or schedules. That is a declared gate, so I do not call it a hidden implemented security failure. It does mean that “off-host backup” and “independent revocation journal” cannot be represented as one scheduled copy from Postgres. Recovery of ordinary content may have a 24-hour RPO; acknowledged access revocations require preserved coverage through the acknowledgment point.

Choose a minimal recovery authority diagram before the hosted slice: separate data backups, journal, key recovery and deadline/task records; name who can append, read and delete each, and how host-loss recovery discovers its complete cutoff. The reaper needs a deadline source it can still access during coordinator loss. Its actual service/IAM cost then joins the budget. The isolated restore and coordinator-loss tests close the implementation gate. Also state whether the native engine is trusted within its coordinator's process/container boundary: a sanitized child environment is a credential-minimization measure, not by itself a demonstrated hostile-process sandbox.

**Diagram-product user — which observable reading task gets better, and will the saved picture retain its meaning and my place?**

The research rightly refuses to equate more glyphs, a shorter layout or deeper root searches with comprehension. It preserves legal check, assessed proxy and uncertainty, and gives the atlas an exact denominator. However, “blinded reader tasks” and “candidate stability” still lack a concrete success criterion. A user cannot infer from six equal SVGs whether they can find the same continuation after restore, distinguish an unassessed check from a supported claim, or explain an atlas frequency edge without mistaking it for candidate quality.

Write three small acceptance tasks before selective-compute implementation: resume a bookmarked alternative and explain its legal history; identify one unassessed versus locally supported check without overclaiming; explain the atlas's visible/collapsed/ended counts and edge legend. Name a baseline and record correctness, time and uncertainty. Define the threshold before comparing equal-budget enrichment. This is a product-evaluation gate, not a reason to withhold the already-supported local serialization work.

## Ranked concrete edits

1. **P1 — connect saved-study occupancy to the resource model.** Add the simple measured-byte sensitivity above, then separate imported encounters, analyzed roots and retained placed revisions in the sizing fixture. Specify a trial saved-study fraction/revision policy and reserve room for WAL, deployment and atomic-save overlap. This is needed before making an operating-cost or retained-library entitlement commitment; it need not block local Slice 1.
2. **P1 — correct the recovery topology.** Draw independently durable revocations separately from scheduled database/artifact backups, and show the reaper plus its independent deadline authority. Add the journal/key/backup principal and cutoff choice as an explicit hosted-slice prerequisite. Preserve 24-hour content RPO without implying a 24-hour revocation-loss allowance.
3. **P2 — scope the next approval to a named deliverable.** Add one sentence authorizing only the local view-save slice as the immediate recommendation, its 4–7 day estimate and its review point; make the later 28–54 day arithmetic and excluded public-product work easy to see. Name view-only versus editable behavior and the supported local-retention contract before coding.
4. **P2 — add the CloudFront DNS billing exception.** The plan guide explicitly says an attached Route 53 zone can move to pay-as-you-go after excess DNS usage and notification; cancelling Free also immediately returns associated resources to pay-as-you-go. Add zone attachment/tier/cancellation drift to reconciliation and keep DNS charges in the fallback model. This is a bounded source correction, not a reason to reject the Free candidate. [AWS plan terms](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonCloudFront/latest/DeveloperGuide/flat-rate-pricing-plan.html).
5. **P2 — make exported diagrams preserve proposal conditions.** Qualify AWS cost/region/size in its title, label the managed runtime alternative, show browser containment in the central flow, label credential retention as conditional and clarify the job retry reconciliation edge. These are documentation edits; no cloud experiment is needed to make them accurate.
6. **P2 — turn diagram quality into three falsifiable reader tasks.** Use the tasks above, predeclare a baseline and success measure, and preserve the result as an experiment gate. Do not promote layout-height reduction or SVG equivalence into a comprehension claim.

## Research adequacy versus remaining gates

The **research is sufficient to start local saved views** after the small handoff decisions above. Reproducible SVG equivalence establishes an unusually clear seam, and the code supports the separation of disposable search cache, immutable retained analysis, placed view and mutable exploration state. The absence of a proved global cache hit rate is acknowledged. Postgres, EC2 and browser-first analysis survive as coherent choices without claiming that their alternatives were disproved.

The **implementation gates remain unrun**: atomic/quota-failure saves, bounded hostile-input validation, supported-browser interaction and peak memory, editable recovery, real Postgres races, full auth/domain boundaries and reader comprehension. The **cloud gates remain unrun**: selected region/host capacity, account-plan acceptance, origin protection/TLS, effective IAM, native worker timing, independent reaping, fresh-host restore and observed charges. Neither set should lower the research verdict merely because this task prohibited building or provisioning them. They do prevent production or public-cost assurances.

The score is **8/10 for the proposal's bounded implementation decision**, not 8/10 production readiness. The two missing points are the unresolved connection between retained value and resource occupancy, and the fact that several visual/operational contracts still depend on caveats located elsewhere. Correct those edges and preserve the existing gate discipline. No broad provider resurvey is required.
